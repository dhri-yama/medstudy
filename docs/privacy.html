MedStudy · Effective [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]

MedStudy is an Android app for practising medical entrance exam questions. It is run by [[OPERATOR NAME]] ("we", "us"), [[OPERATOR ADDRESS]]. This page explains what the app stores about you, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Email address, and your name if you sign in with GoogleTo create your account and sign you in.
A password (stored only in scrambled form), if you sign up with emailTo sign you in. We cannot read it.
Profile: display name, the exams you chose, your daily question goalTo set up the app for you and restore it on another phone.
Your answers: which question, which option you picked, whether it was right, when, and your time zoneTo show your progress and streak and keep them when you change phones.
Question reports you sendTo fix mistakes in questions.
Subscription status and the Google Play purchase referenceTo know whether you have a trial, a paid plan or the free plan.
Sign-in sessions: when each device signed in, with its IP address and device typeTo keep you signed in and to limit an account to 2 devices at a time.

Bookmarks, your weekly study plan and reminder settings stay on your phone and are not sent to us.

We do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your information.

App permissions

Display over other apps is used only if you turn on the question pop-up. It lets MedStudy show a question on top of the screen. It does not read or record what is in other apps.

Notifications are used for the question pop-up service and reminders.

Who else handles your information

ServiceWhat it does for us
SupabaseHosts the database and handles sign-in. Your account, profile and answers are stored there.
Google Sign-InIf you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms who you are and gives us your email and name.
Google Play BillingTakes payment for subscriptions. We never see your card or bank details.

These services may store information on servers outside your country. We share your information with nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your email, profile, answers, reports, subscription record and sign-in sessions are deleted from our database straight away.

One thing is kept after deletion: a scrambled, one-way code made from your email address, together with the date your free trial started. It cannot be turned back into your email. We keep it only so that the free trial is not given again to the same email.

Purchase records held by Google Play are kept by Google under its own policy.

Your choices

  • See or correct your information: your profile can be changed in the app under Me. For anything else, write to us.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Me, then Account, then Delete account. You can also ask us from the account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent or complain: write to us at the address below. If you are in India, you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Information travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted connection. Each account can read only its own information. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects your information, as the law requires.

Children

MedStudy is meant for medical students and graduates. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, where we can, tell you in the app or by email.
