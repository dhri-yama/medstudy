In the app

  1. Open MedStudy and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my MedStudy account". We will delete it within [[NUMBER]] days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your email address, name, profile, answers and progress, question reports, subscription record and sign-in sessions.

What is kept

A scrambled, one-way code made from your email address, with the date your free trial started. It cannot be turned back into your email. It is kept only so that the free trial is not given again to the same email.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions, or you will keep being charged.
